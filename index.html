☕ KINTO press recipe

  1. Grind: Ask for a coarse French-press grind.
  2. Measure: Add about 10g of grounds (roughly 2 level tablespoons; use the mug's coffee marking if present).
  3. Heat: Boil water in your Jetboil, then wait about 30 seconds.
  4. Pour: Add hot water to the mug's safe fill mark (start around 200ml, adjusting for your mug).
  5. Steep: Wait 4 minutes, then press the filter down slowly.
  6. Enjoy: Drink carefully — the mug and coffee will be hot.
